# 🏰 Realms of Myth 1.0.0 — Ascension

The full release of the Realms of Myth fantasy RPG add-on for Minecraft Bedrock & Education Edition. Everything below is in the box: custom art, particles, audio, and game systems — no vanilla placeholders left behind.

## ✨ New in 1.0.0 "Ascension"

- **📜 Chronicles of the Realm** — an 8-quest progressive chain (kills, collects, crafting & boss milestones) with loot, XP, titles and sound stings. Track it with `!quest`.
- **🗡️ Mythic weapon tier (T5)** — ☀️ Dawnbreaker (radiant AoE smite), 🌑 Void Reaver (shadow-step behind your target), ⚡ Stormcaller Hammer (a real lightning bolt on every hit). Crafted from Nether Star cores, Dragon Hearts and Mythril.
- **🐉 Dragon taming & riding** — weaken a Fire or Frost Dragon below 30% HP, win its loyalty with Mythril Ingots, saddle up and steer with your view. Breath attack on demand; whelps can imprint as pets.
- **🔮 Ancient Altar rituals & class prestige** — sacrifice a Dragon Heart + 4 Mythril to summon three elite guardians; defeat them for a guaranteed Mythic weapon. Prestige at level 30+ for permanent stacking max HP and an eternal class-colored aura.
- **🌍 Difficulty scaling** — hostile mobs grow tougher the farther you roam from spawn (×1.0 / ×1.5 / ×2.25).

## 🎨 Fully Custom Presentation

- **14 hand-tuned particle effects** — fireball trails, frost nova rings, arcane steps, holy light beams, dragon breaths and more. Every vanilla placeholder particle in every ability and boss fight has been replaced.
- **A complete synthesized audio suite** — 41 production-quality WAVs across 45+ sound events: ability SFX, mob voices, UI stings, weapon impacts, and an original dragon-boss battle track.
- **Full mob animations** for all custom entities, and **all 28 armor pieces fully visible when worn** (layers + attachables).
- **Procedural art pipeline** — every texture, promo banner and showcase grid in this release is generated by deterministic PIL pixel-art generators (see `texture-sources/` in the repo). The promo images are texture showcases, not in-game screenshots.

## 🎮 The Core Game (since 0.x)

- 4 playable races (Elf, Troll, Giant, Human) and 5 classes (Mage, Ranger, Berserker, Paladin, Druid) with 15 unique abilities
- 5 weapon tiers and 4 armor tiers, including per-class Class Master set bonuses
- Fire & Frost dragon bosses with 3-phase AI (ground → aerial → enraged, whelp adds)
- Ancient Altar structure, Class Tokens, custom blocks, structures and NPC dialogue

## 📦 Install

1. Download `realms-of-myth.mcaddon` below (or from the repo)
2. Double-click it — Minecraft imports both packs automatically
3. Create a world → Add-Ons → activate both packs
4. Enable **Beta APIs** under Experiments
5. `!class` to begin. What destiny will you forge in the realms?
